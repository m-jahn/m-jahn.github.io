We recently noticed problems to map next generation sequencing reads to certain regions of the *Streptococcus* genome, our pet organism for the study of pathogenic bacteria at the MPUSP. An extensive re-sequencing effort using short and long read methods revealed that the original assembly contained various errors, particularly for repetitive rRNA/tRNA operons (published now in [MRA](https://doi.org/10.1128/mra.01197-24)). Our new assembly fixes these errors and adds two new tRNAs, becoming the current reference assembly for *Streptococcus pyogenes*. It's available on [NCBI datasets](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/genome/GCF_043231225.1/).
